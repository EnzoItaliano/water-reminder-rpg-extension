# Privacy Policy for HydroQuest 🛡️

**Last Updated:** March 14, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. **HydroQuest** is designed with a "privacy-first" approach. This policy explains in full detail how the extension collects, processes, stores, and shares your data, in compliance with the Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies.

---

## 1. Data Collection

HydroQuest collects only the data necessary to operate its core features. Data collection is split into two categories: **local-only** (always active) and **cloud** (only when you create an account and log in).

### 1.1 Local Data (Always Collected)

The following data is generated by your interactions and stored locally on your device via `chrome.storage.local`. It is collected regardless of whether you are logged in:

| Data Type | Details |
|---|---|
| **Hydration Data** | Water intake goal (ml), number of cups logged, and timestamps of each drink within a session |
| **Session Data** | Session duration, start time, result (won/lost), difficulty level, and chosen monster ID |
| **RPG Progress** | Player level, XP, gold, unlocked monsters, and trophy history (date, monster, difficulty) |
| **User Settings** | Notification preferences and last selected monster |
| **Device ID** | A randomly generated UUID (created locally, never linked to your identity without login) |
| **Last Synced Snapshot** | A copy of the last game state that was pushed to the cloud (used to calculate sync deltas) |

### 1.2 Account & Cloud Data (Only When You Register/Log In)

Cloud features are **entirely optional**. If you choose to create an account, the following additional data is collected and stored by Google Firebase on our behalf:

| Data Type | Details |
|---|---|
| **Email Address** | Collected at registration and used for authentication via Firebase Auth |
| **Password** | Stored securely as a hash by Firebase Auth; we never have access to your raw password |
| **Game Progress (Cloud Copy)** | Level, XP, trophies, unlocked monsters, total water drank (ml), sessions completed, total cups, and bank gold are pushed to Firestore when you manually trigger a sync |
| **Device Metadata** | Your device ID (UUID) and a `lastUpdated` timestamp are stored in Firestore to help with multi-device sync logic |

We do **not** collect:
- Your name, phone number, or any other personal identifier beyond your email
- Your browsing history or activity on any websites
- Precise geolocation data
- Health or biometric data beyond what you manually enter

## 2. Data Processing

### 2.1 Local Processing
All core game logic runs entirely on your device. The extension processes your local data to:
- Calculate your daily progress toward your water intake goal
- Trigger RPG events (battles, level-ups, gold rewards) based on your hydration habits
- Schedule reminder alarms at intervals calculated from your session settings
- Display notifications when a reminder is due or a session ends

### 2.2 Cloud Processing (Accounts Only)
When you manually trigger a sync, the extension:
1. Reads the latest data from Firestore (cloud)
2. Merges it with your local data using a delta-based strategy (cumulative stats like total water are summed across devices)
3. Saves the merged result locally and pushes it back to Firestore

No automated or background cloud processing occurs. All sync operations are initiated explicitly by you.

---

## 3. Data Storage

### 3.1 Local Storage
All local data is stored using the `chrome.storage.local` API. This means:
- Your data **remains on your device** at all times
- It is **not synced** to any Google Account or browser sync
- It is **not accessible** to any website or third-party service
- Uninstalling the extension typically causes the browser to remove all associated local data

### 3.2 Cloud Storage (Accounts Only)
If you create an account, your game data and account credentials are stored in Google Firebase services:
- **Firebase Authentication** — securely stores your email and hashed password
- **Cloud Firestore** — stores your synced game progress and device metadata

Firebase is a service provided by Google LLC. For information on how Google stores and protects this data, please refer to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Firebase's data processing terms](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

Your data in Firestore is stored under your unique Firebase User ID (`uid`) and is only accessible by the authenticated user.

---

## 4. Data Sharing

HydroQuest does **not** sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties for commercial purposes. The only data transmission that occurs is between your browser and Google Firebase, solely for the purpose of enabling the optional cloud sync and authentication features described in this policy.

Specifically:
- We do **not** share data with advertisers or analytics providers
- We do **not** share data with any government or law enforcement entity, except where required by law
- We do **not** share data with other extensions or applications
- The only third-party service involved is **Google Firebase**, used exclusively for user authentication and game data sync

---

## 5. Permissions

HydroQuest requests the following browser permissions:

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `storage` | Saves your hydration goals, game progress, device ID, and settings locally on your device |
| `alarms` | Schedules periodic hydration reminder checks and session timeout detection in the background |
| `notifications` | Displays drink reminders and session result alerts to you |

The extension also declares the following **host permissions** to communicate with Firebase:

| Host | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `https://*.firebaseio.com/*` | Firestore database communication for cloud sync |
| `https://*.googleapis.com/*` | Google APIs used by Firebase Authentication |
| `https://*.firebaseapp.com/*` | Firebase Authentication endpoints |

These host permissions are only used when you are logged in and trigger a sync. No communication with these hosts occurs if you are not logged in.

---

## 6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data:

- **Local data** is only readable and writable by the extension itself through the `chrome.storage.local` API
- **Passwords** are never stored or transmitted in plain text; Firebase Auth handles all credential management
- **Cloud data** is stored under your unique Firebase UID, which is only accessible to authenticated sessions
- The extension does **not** execute remote code; all logic is bundled locally
- No sensitive tokens or credentials are stored in plain text in local storage

## 7. Children's Privacy

HydroQuest is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has created an account and you have concerns, please contact us using the information below.

---

## 8. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

- **Access & Portability:** Your local data is accessible in your browser storage at any time
- **Deletion (Local):** You can reset your local data from the Options page, or by uninstalling the extension
- **Deletion (Cloud Account):** To delete your Firebase account and all associated cloud data, please contact us via the GitHub repository. We will permanently delete your Firestore document and Firebase Auth record
- **Correction:** You can modify your local game data by resetting it from the Options page
